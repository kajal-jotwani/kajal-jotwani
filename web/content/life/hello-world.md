Every developer rewrites their portfolio about five times before shipping it. This is my (checks git log) *n*-th attempt — and the first one that felt like me.

Here's the thing: I love backends and systems, but I also love the moment code stops being useful and starts being *beautiful*. Generative art. Live-coded music. The whole creative-coding world where a for-loop can be a brush stroke.

So this site is both. On the surface it's a normal portfolio — projects, timeline, blogs, a way to reach me. But:

- the background is a **flow field** — hundreds of particles following an invisible noise grid, painting as they go
- if you turn on the ♪ in the corner, the page becomes an instrument: keystrokes play an e-piano, and every project card has **its own drum groove**, generated from its name
- there's a live-codeable version of the site's score at [/play](/play)

Everything I write about tech still lives on my [Medium](https://medium.com/@kajaljotwani06). This space is for the softer stuff — what I'm learning, where I'm going, and the occasional bug that deserves a eulogy.

Thanks for stopping by. Turn the sound on before you leave. ♪
